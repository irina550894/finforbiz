## Modal behavior

Active cards open a fullscreen modal gallery:

- darkened backdrop;
- close button;
- previous and next controls;
- image counter;
- image caption;
- keyboard support: `Esc`, `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`;
- pointer swipe support for mobile.

## Stage 5 status

The portfolio block satisfies Stage 5:

- the block is visible on the main page;
- categories are shown as dashboard-style cards;
- calculators and models have real visual examples;
- reports and automation have placeholders;
- the structure can accept more portfolio items later.

Dynamic JSON-driven updates are intentionally left for Stage 6.
